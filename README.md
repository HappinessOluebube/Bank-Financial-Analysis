Overview
The Campaign Analysis Dashboard evaluates marketing performance for January 2025, tracking clicks, conversions, leads, revenue, and platform effectiveness.

Key Insights
1. Impressions: Reached 1.25 million.
2. Conversions: Increased by 20.6% to 2,947.
3. Platform Performance: Facebook generated the highest share of clicks at 32.4%.
4. Revenue: Grew by 28.4% to $48,720.
   
Recommendations
1. Increase Reach: Optimize campaigns to attract more relevant audiences.
2. Improve Conversions: Enhance landing pages and calls to action.
3. Optimize Ad Spend: Prioritize platforms delivering better results.
4. Track Profitability: Compare revenue and ad spend to measure ROAS.
